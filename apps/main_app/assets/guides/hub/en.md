# What is the Hub

The Hub is a library of ready-made resources for quests. If you do not want to hunt for a background, music and sounds yourself, start with the Hub.

## What is inside

- **Backgrounds** — images and textures for screens.
- **Music** — tracks for scenes and menus.
- **Sounds** — short effects: taps, steps, hits.
- **Fonts** — text styling options.
- **Button presets** — ready-made button looks.

Resources are added by the community and the Meander team. The same background can serve several quests — that is fine.

## How to add a resource

1. Open the Hub from **My library**: the storefront icon in the top right corner.
2. Pick a section and find what you need.
3. Press the button that adds it to your library.

Everything you add appears in **My library** and becomes available in the quest editor: when you pick an image, music or font.

## Why it helps

- You do not have to draw a background just to test an idea.
- Resources already fit the format and do not break the quest build.
- The library works offline: downloads stay on the device.

> Tip: start with one background, one music track and one tap sound. That is enough to make a scene feel alive.
