# How the market works

The market is the quest showcase. So a newcomer does not drown in thousands of releases, the catalog is split into three levels.

## Featured

The first level holds quests the team picked by hand. These are finished games: a story, tested links and tidy screens. They sit at the top of the feed and are open to everyone.

If you have just started playing Meander, begin here. You will see what a finished quest looks like and what to aim for.

## Moderated

The second level holds releases that passed moderation and were published. They are full quests that did not make the hand-picked selection. You can play them too, and ratings and reviews help authors reach the first level.

## Demos

The third level is demo quests. They are short, show what the editor can do and serve as examples. Demos stay out of the main feed: they should not compete with full games.

## What it means for a player

- Start with featured: those are curated works.
- If you like an author, look at their other quests.
- Rate what you play: ratings move good quests up.

## What it means for an author

- A demo is a showcase of possibilities, not a shortcut to the top.
- A full quest goes through moderation and lands on the second level.
- Careful work with a story and tested links has a chance to be picked for the featured shelf.
