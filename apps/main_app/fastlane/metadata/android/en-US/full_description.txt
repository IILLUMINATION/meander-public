Meander is a free engine for text quests and interactive stories. You can play
them, and you can write your own without knowing how to program.

For players:
• branching scenes with variables, inventory and tables;
• images, sound and video inside a scene, text animation, background blur;
• a community catalogue with discussions and reviews;
• offline mode: a quest is a file on your device and needs no network.

For authors:
• a node graph, so the shape of the story is visible;
• a layered canvas for scene layout;
• visual script blocks instead of code, formulas in plain fields;
• a resource library, HTML and PDF export;
• publishing to the catalogue, view statistics and reviews.

The editor, the player and the quest library run locally with no account and
no network. An account is only needed for cloud sync, publishing, forums and
direct messages; those are served by meander.illuminat.dev, whose backend lives in this
repository (crates/mnd-server), so you can host your own.
